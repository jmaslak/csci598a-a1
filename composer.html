      What goes in a first entry

      • What's happened so far, in plain words.
      • Who you are, if some readers won't know.
      • How often you expect to post.
      • What would actually help right now.

      You're not being graded

      Short is fine. Two sentences is fine. People are here because they care about you, not because they're reading closely for style.

      About this page

      A working demo of a voice-first way to write a journal entry. It borrows CaringBridge's look, but it isn't CaringBridge and it doesn't connect to any account.
